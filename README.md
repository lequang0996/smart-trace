## 🇬🇧 English

**Title (30/30):** SmartTrace: Label Traceability

**Short description (76/80):** Scan labels, barcodes and QR: see specs, origin and check label consistency.

**Full description (2963/4000):**

```text
SmartTrace is an all-in-one traceability app. Take or choose a photo of a product label, carton, packaging or barcode and the app reads what is printed on it and tells you whether the label is consistent or has points worth checking.

WORKS EVEN WITHOUT A BARCODE
Many products (ceramic tiles, tile adhesive, cement, boxed imports…) have no barcode, only printed text, Chinese characters and even handwriting. SmartTrace reads text on your phone (Latin and Chinese), turns sideways photos upright and extracts fields such as size, quantity, area, weight, grade, item code, colour shade, batch, production date, expiry, standards, manufacturer and origin.

FIVE CLEAR TABS
• Overview: status and consistency score of the label.
• Specifications: every detail the app could read.
• Authenticity: arithmetic checks (pieces × size = area), barcodes, QR codes and database comparison.
• Supply chain: origin, manufacturer, batch and dates, barcode registry.
• Legal & certificates: expiry, standards printed on the label, Vietnamese sub-label check (when the app is set to Vietnamese).

BARCODES AND QR CODES
If a barcode or QR is visible, the app validates the GS1 check digit and country prefix, and offers a button that opens Verified by GS1 to see which company the code is licensed to. For QR codes with links it only shows the domain and warns about shortened, unencrypted or look-alike links; it never opens a link by itself.

TWO MODES
• Offline: everything is processed on your phone and the photo is not uploaded.
• Gemini AI (optional): uses your own Gemini API key to read handwriting and mixed-language labels better. Add several keys (one per line, shown masked), automatic fallback to the next key when one hits its limit, and an in-app guide to creating a key. A photo is sent to Google only when you switch this mode on and agree.

FAST TEXT-TO-SPEECH
Listen to the result summary and the user guide with your phone’s voice. Default speed 2×, up to 4×, with the paragraph being read highlighted.

HISTORY AND FAVORITES
Every scan is saved privately on your phone. Tap the heart to keep important products.

ELEGANT LIGHT AND DARK DESIGN
Navy and gold, light/dark/system themes and a built-in user guide.

8 LANGUAGES
English · 简体中文 · Español · Português · Русский · 日本語 · 한국어 · Tiếng Việt

PRIVACY
No account. No camera, location or contacts permission. History stays on your device. The app never sends barcode digits by itself; only when you tap “Verify on GS1” does your browser open the GS1 page. The app shows Google AdMob ads and a consent form where required by law.

IMPORTANT
Results are for reference only. A photo cannot prove a product is genuine, and “not found in a database” does not mean fake. Confirm with the manufacturer or an authorized seller before buying or using a product. The app is independent and not affiliated with any brand or authority.

Contact: 17eathday@gmail.com · Developer: FWs
```
